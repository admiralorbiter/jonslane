Laboratory B — Concept & Methodology

Discovery Compression

A computational investigation of the earliest possible dates for scientific discoveries, and what the pattern of delays reveals about what we cannot yet see.

The Formal Core

For a theory T and a calendar year y, define the historical capability inventory Ay — a structured description of what instruments, materials, mathematics, and institutional organization existed in that year. The discovery horizon is:

y*(T) = min y [ ∃ experiment e ∈ Ay such that e strongly discriminates T ]

y*(T) is not the year someone could have developed the complete modern theory. It is the earliest point when they could have obtained compelling evidence for one of its important structural claims — using only what was available at that time.
