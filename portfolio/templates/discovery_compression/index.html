Laboratory B — Discovery Compression

When Could We Have Known?

For each true modern theory, find the earliest year when a rigorous investigator — using only the tools, observations, and mathematics of that time, but knowing where to look — could have obtained compelling evidence.

Not asked: “Given the old theory, why did it fail?”
Asked instead: “Given the true theory, how far back in history could part of it have been discovered, provided the investigator knew where to look?”

The Three Discovery Dates

First Observable

When the relevant phenomenon could have been noticed with existing instruments — even if misclassified.

First Testable

When available tools could distinguish competing explanations with adequate precision. The discovery horizon y*(T).

First Comprehensible

When the mathematical or conceptual framework existed to correctly interpret the result. Often centuries later.

The gap between First Testable and Actual Discovery is the compression gap — the measure of how much earlier a discovery could have happened.

Four System Components

Historical Capability Model Ay

For a selected year, a structured inventory of instruments, materials, mathematics, and institutional organization — including noise floors, tolerances, and social constraints. What was measurable, buildable, and computable?

Theory Compiler

Translates the modern theory into observable predictions that do not require modern instruments or vocabulary. Traces: hidden mechanism → intermediate process → period-measurable observable.

Experiment Search

Searches the historically feasible experiment space. Scores each candidate by: expected discrimination among theories, divided by cost + duration + measurement difficulty. Finds the cheapest experiment that changes what a reasonable observer should believe.

Hindsight-Leak Detector

Audits every proposed experiment for smuggled modern knowledge: anachronistic materials, modern statistical conventions, calibration dependencies on later science, and hidden spatial or conceptual maps only available in hindsight.

Discovery Compression is a companion laboratory to Experimental Archaeology. Where Archaeology asks “how did a wrong theory explain the world?” — this laboratory asks “when was the right answer first reachable?”
