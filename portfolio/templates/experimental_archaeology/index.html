{# ── Methodology ───────────────────────────────────────── #}

Four Reconstruction Modes

Historically Faithful

Only the concepts, mathematics, parameters, and observations available at the time. Asks why intelligent people accepted this — and which anomalies were already visible.

Charitable Modern

Modern mathematics formalizes ambiguous historical claims, while preserving the theory's original ontology. The strongest coherent version — not a straw man.

Best-Fit

An optimizer tunes every parameter the theory legitimately possesses. Distinguishes a theory that fundamentally cannot work from one that merely had poor historical measurements.

Counterfactual

Historically plausible modifications only. Could this theory have been repaired without abandoning its central worldview? What would that repair have cost it?
