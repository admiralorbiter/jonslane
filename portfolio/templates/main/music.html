djon's Blurbs

About me:

Hey! I'm djon (pronounced like Dijon, or DJ-on, or just Jon with a silent D). I'm a bedroom mixer turned event DJ who is absolutely obsessed with the music that shaped our generation. I specialize in curated mixes that feature hip hop, pop, and millennial throwbacks.

For me, DJing isn't just about playing hit songs. It's about triggering memories—mixing that one tracks from your middle school dance with a classic hip hop bassline from MTV.

Event Booking & DJ Services:

I love spinning for private events, house parties, club nights, and corporate gatherings. If you need a DJ who knows how to mix, who will actually read a room, and keep people dancing instead, let's talk!
