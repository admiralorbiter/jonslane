# DJ Roomba — Research Citations

- **Foster, Beffa & Lehmann (2011)**: Professional DJs show enhanced tempo judgment in 120–139 BPM range. Underpins tempo compatibility scoring dimensions.
- **Levitin & Cook (1996)**: 72% of recalled tempos are within 8% of original tempo. Supports anchor-song felt tempo memory structures.
- **Klapuri, Eronen & Astola (2006)**: Meter operates at multiple levels (tatum, tactus, measure). Metrical double/half-time matchups are mathematically defined as valid transitions.
- **Ellis (2007)**: Librosa uses Ellis-style onset estimation for beat-tracking.
- **Serato Docs**: Manual correction is standard in professional DJ software. Auto-estimations represent high/low machine confidence, inviting manual overrides.
- **Open Music Theory 2.0**: Phrase form comprises units with a trajectory towards a goal. Phrase alignment is a scored transition quality.
